Your data in Reader

Privacy information for Reader 1.0, including local learning, optional external services and additional feedback when using TestFlight.

Updated: 2026-10-07 · Sergii Tenditnyi

Contact and scope

Reader is provided by Sergii Tenditnyi. Contact tenditniy@gmail.com for privacy questions or requests concerning information held by the publisher.

This policy covers Reader 1.0 and its TestFlight beta. Build134 uses individually configured external services. The next gateway-enabled build also offers optional automatic Reader AI access described below. Reading and offline vocabulary practice are free. No paid Reader subscription or user account registration is required. Services you configure yourself retain their own operators and terms.

Data on your device

Reader stores imported books and their images, reading positions, bookmarks, saved words and meanings, learning history, drafts, book conversations, settings and saved audio. These support reading, search, playback and practice.

Kokoro generates speech on the device. Dictation uses Apple's on-device recognition; initial language-resource downloads may require a network. AI keys are stored in Keychain separately for each recipient and excluded from Reader backups.

Local vocabulary practice

For saved words with a confirmed meaning, Reader continues practice without cloud AI. The application stage checks recall of a known word in saved context or from its meaning. The answer, result and progress are saved on the device; the answer is not sent to an AI service for this check. When sync is enabled, learning history is also part of your iCloud data.

Your own practice sentence and the local answer are stored separately. A local word check does not assess whether your sentence uses the word correctly; the draft remains available for a separate check when cloud service is available and you have authorized sharing.

iCloud and portable backups

When your iCloud is available, automatic sync, enabled by default, sends books, original images, positions, words, learning history, bookmarks and core settings to your iCloud Drive. Saved mnemonic images or their references are sent with the words. To reconcile changes, Reader also stores its generated sync-device identifier and word-deletion records in iCloud. You can disable sync in iCloud & Backups.

Portable backups include books, positions, words and core settings. Generated audio, models, custom voices and API keys are excluded from Reader backups. Older backups are not automatically deleted. Copies that you save or share separately remain under your control.

First launch and permission

On the first ordinary launch, Reader shows the current HTTPS-service recipients and data categories in one window if their decision has not yet been saved. Text, images and remote narration can be selected separately. Accept authorizes only the enabled services; Not now grants no permission. Opening the window or leaving a switch on before confirmation does not authorize a request.

The choice is saved for the specific recipient and disclosure version. Existing permissions remain in place; changing an address does not authorize sharing with a new recipient. Reading and practice continue without visiting settings. Permissions remain available later in AI and privacy settings.

Automatic Reader AI service

In a gateway-enabled build, Reader can connect automatically without a provider API key. Each service still requires your explicit permission. Requests pass through Reader AI Gateway hosted on ChatGPT Sites using OpenAI/Cloudflare infrastructure. Text and mnemonic-image requests then go to OpenRouter and its model-serving operators. Remote narration and alignment go to the publisher-operated Qwen server at reader654.duckdns.org. You may instead choose your own compatible HTTPS services.

Apple App Attest verifies the application and creates a device-bound key. Reader retains its key reference in Keychain; its short-lived service token stays in memory. The gateway retains an installation identifier derived from that reference, the attested public key and assertion counter, registration time, temporary challenge/session records and access counters. The identifier is persistent and linked to that installation; hashing does not make it anonymous. These records support authentication, abuse prevention, access isolation and usage limits. Challenges expire after two minutes, sessions after thirty minutes and access counters by their quota period; expired temporary records are removed during subsequent authentication. Registration records have no automatic deletion deadline.

The gateway relays request content and generated results without storing books or audio in its application database. Hosting infrastructure can process connection metadata such as IP address, route, time, response status and request headers. OpenRouter and downstream operators apply their own retention and processing terms; no universal zero-retention or no-training promise is made. Qwen retains requested text, jobs, generated audio, client and book identifiers and listening metadata to support resumable processing and delivery. Disposable-cache cleanup does not erase all retained jobs or identifiers. Contact the publisher for access or deletion requests concerning this service. The service does not use these records for advertising, tracking across companies or sale to data brokers. For a support request, include the non-secret service identifier shown in AI and privacy settings after your first connection.

Each installation has isolated Qwen access and cannot retrieve another installation's books, audio or saved voices. The service has finite request and spending limits. Qwen depends on the publisher's compute server being reachable; saved audio, local Kokoro and ordinary vocabulary practice remain available when the service fails.

Cloud text AI

After permission, the selected text service receives the words, book excerpts, questions and conversation history, meanings and practice sentences needed for the requested feature. Locally dictated text may be sent for a cloud assessment. Local word practice does not send its answer for that assessment. The current recipient is shown when granting permission and in settings.

For book answers and summaries, the service may receive retrieved passages, current context, saved book knowledge and previous replies. Already-read portions of a book may be sent successively in ranges to create a summary; sharing is not limited to a single selected excerpt.

Automatic Reader access uses OpenRouter with the GLM model. Individually configured text access can use the Z.ai API or another compatible endpoint. Z.ai publishes an API data-processing addendum distinct from its consumer-chat policy. Your account terms and selected service must be considered separately.

Mnemonic images

After separate permission, OpenRouter receives a word, meaning and book sentence to create a mnemonic prompt, then the generated or user-edited prompt for the image. Requests specify OpenAI and Google model labels. The serving operator depends on the route selected by the service; a model label alone does not identify that operator.

OpenRouter and the downstream operator process requests under their applicable terms and account settings. Reader does not promise zero retention across all recipients. Review the privacy information for the service and route you choose.

External image search

When Wikimedia image search is used, the search term is sent to Wikimedia Commons. Loading an external image contacts its source server, which receives ordinary network information including the IP address. This is separate from AI image generation.

Remote Qwen narration

After permission, a configured Qwen HTTPS server with an access key receives narration text and preceding context, book metadata, voice settings and book/chunk/cache/job identifiers. Preparing a whole book transmits its text. Coordination of generation and delivery also sends a persistent client UUID generated by Reader, its generated display label and listening-state metadata. This is not your personal iPhone name; the server receives ordinary network information including the IP address.

Alignment uploads generated speech audio as WAV together with its source text and language. This is synthesized speech, not the microphone recording from local dictation; a separate cloud check of a dictated answer may send its text.

On the publisher-operated Qwen server, automatic cleanup runs during idle maintenance. Job manifests and their delivery ZIP copies become eligible one hour after verified receipt, seven days after cancellation, or thirty days without an update for paused, failed or completed jobs. Active processing and transfers are protected; cleanup can be delayed while the service is busy or unavailable. Invalid retention metadata is retained for investigation. Disposable unpinned audio cache has a six-hour idle threshold; generated quarantine copies have a seven-day threshold. These rules do not erase copies on your iPhone, saved voices, installation/access registration, administrative backups or support records, for which no automatic deletion deadline is promised. After an idle server job expires, resuming it can require generating a new copy. Contact the publisher for access or deletion requests. Personally configured servers follow their selected operator's rules.

Diagnostics, support and this site

Reader's detailed diagnostic journal is local and is not automatically sent to support. Detailed journaling is off on ordinary Release launches unless enabled for diagnosis. Review any journal before sharing it.

TestFlight is separate from Reader's local journal. When you install Reader through TestFlight, Apple and the application provider automatically receive crash logs and usage information through TestFlight. This collection cannot be disabled within TestFlight. You may also choose to send written feedback, screenshots and crash comments; these can contain personal information. The publisher uses TestFlight information to improve Reader and related products, under Apple's TestFlight rules.

Email support receives your email address, message and attachments you choose to send. The support address uses Gmail. This information is used to answer your request and investigate Reader problems. No automatic deletion deadline is promised for support correspondence; you can request deletion at the contact address, subject to applicable legal obligations. Do not send API keys, passwords or complete private books.

These pages are prepared for GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security. The pages contain no analytics, forms, scripts, external fonts or other external assets. Following a source link contacts that source; an email link opens your email application.

Subscriptions and purchase data

No live managed Reader cloud subscription or paid purchase is active in this version. Reader does not collect payment-card details through these pages.

Using an external service with your own credentials is subject to your agreement with that operator. Permission to send text or audio is separate from any operator's billing. The optional automatic Reader service is described above and has usage limits; no paid Reader subscription is active.

Your choices and deletion

Withdraw AI permissions or remove keys in AI settings. Withdrawal stops new requests and cancels active requests, but cancellation cannot recall data already sent.

Library materials and saved words are removed separately. Local removal does not clear older iCloud backups or external-service copies. Contact support about access, correction or deletion of data held by Reader; contact the operator for a service you configure yourself. Specific rights and lawful exceptions depend on applicable law.
