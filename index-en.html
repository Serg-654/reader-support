Read. Listen. Remember.

Help with Reader, your books, narration and vocabulary practice.

Updated: 2026-10-04 · Sergii Tenditnyi

Contact us

Email tenditniy@gmail.com for Reader support, feedback or questions about data processing. Include the app version, iPhone model and what happened. You may attach a screenshot after removing personal information.

Do not send passwords, AI API keys or complete private books. If a diagnostic file is needed, we will first agree on what it should contain.

Without internet or AI access

You can use already imported books, local dictionaries, Kokoro, saved audio and offline vocabulary practice. Reading and offline practice remain free.

When cloud AI is unavailable, practice for saved words with a confirmed meaning continues in the same queue. In the application stage, fill in the missing word from saved context or recall it from its meaning, press Check, then Next word. Reader checks the known answer on the device and saves the review result.

Your own sentence remains a separate draft: the local word check does not assign it an AI assessment. New cloud translations, images, answers and remote narration require an available service and permission to share data.

Importing books and listening

Reader supports TXT, EPUB and FB2 imports. Make the source file available in Files, then select it using Reader's import control.

Choose Kokoro for local narration. Remote Qwen requires a separately configured HTTPS server, permission and an access key; previously saved audio can play without a new request to it. For audio or import problems, send reproduction steps without attaching a private book.

Sync and backups

iCloud & Backups provides automatic-sync controls, backup creation and merging a backup into your library. Automatic sync is enabled by default when iCloud is available.

Removing a book on a device does not mean saved words, older backups or data already sent to an AI service are removed. Prepare a separate backup before making important changes.

AI permissions and data requests

At first launch, the AI in Reader window shows the current recipients and the data they receive. Choose services and press Accept, or continue with Not now. Data sharing is authorized only for the selected services after you press Accept; reading and practice are available with either choice.

Later, change or withdraw text, image and remote-speech permissions in AI and privacy settings. Visiting settings is not required to continue practice. Changing a service address does not transfer permission to a new recipient. Withdrawal stops new transmissions; it does not delete copies already received by a service.

For a data request, email support and identify the feature concerned. Access keys are not required. If you configure your own service, its operator is responsible for data held by that service.

This support website

This support site is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub logs and stores visitors’ IP addresses for security. Reader’s support pages contain no analytics, forms, scripts or external fonts. The email link opens your email app.
